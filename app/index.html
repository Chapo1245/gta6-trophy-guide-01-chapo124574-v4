    Roadmap to Platinum

    Six phases, in order. The story first — everything in Phase 1 pops on its own — then the stranger strands while they are still open, the missable sweep, collectibles, activities, and finally money and the 100% checklist. PowerPyx puts the whole road at about 200 hours; Phase 6 alone is around 100 of them.

      No mission replay: keep a separate save at the start and end of each chapter (PowerPyx suggests ten slots rotated this way). After the story, the 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — and it is lenient, closer to 60% true completion: only 70 of the 80 Challenges, 200 of 277 vehicle models and 100 of 255 animals are needed.

      Missables — 15 trophies to do before they close

      GTA 6 has no mission replay, so a strand or moment left behind can stay behind. These fifteen are flagged from the official descriptions and the cited guides. Do them as they appear; post-story free-roam cannot reopen a closed strand.

      One more warning — Positive Mental Attitude. PowerPyx flags the first meditation as the only missable step in the whole game: in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning, follow her to the balcony and meditate. Miss it and the 18-spot trophy is at risk. It is not counted among the fifteen above because the other seventeen spots are all reachable later — but the Chapter 1 one is not.

        Collectibles — five sets, five target numbers

        Collectibles are the long grind behind the platinum. Each card below gives the target, what counts toward it, and which trophies it feeds.

        Also on the 100% checklist: 33 underwater discoveries, 100 Cocaine Flamingos, 15 of the 18 card sets, and studying 100 of 255 animals through binoculars (PowerPyx). Scan everything with WAiNK as you go — a scan is often all a discovery needs.
